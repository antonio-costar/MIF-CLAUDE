# Business Problem and Goal

## Background

The Adult Income dataset contains demographic, educational, and employment information about individuals.

The target variable indicates whether a person earns more than $50,000 per year.

## Business Question

What characteristics are most strongly associated with earning more than $50K annually?

## Objectives

1. Understand the overall composition of the population.
2. Identify which demographic and employment attributes are associated with higher income.
3. Determine the strongest predictors of earning more than $50K.
4. Develop insights that could support future predictive modeling efforts.
5. Recommend data preparation and feature engineering opportunities.

## Analytical Goals

The analysis should:

- Assess data quality.
- Identify missing values and anomalies.
- Explore relationships between features and income level.
- Evaluate differences across demographic groups.
- Generate clear visualizations and business insights.
- Highlight variables that may be useful for predictive modeling.

## Success Criteria

A successful analysis should answer:

- Who is most likely to earn more than $50K?
- Which variables appear to be the strongest predictors?
- What data quality issues should be addressed?
- What preprocessing steps are required before modeling?
- What new features might improve predictive performance?

## Expected Output

The final deliverable should include:

- Data quality assessment
- Exploratory data analysis
- Key visualizations
- Summary of findings
- Recommendations for feature engineering
- Recommendations for machine learning model development
